# Pricing

## Tier 1: Starter - $49

Best for: Individual developers, students

Includes:
- Full source code
- Commercial license (single developer)
- Documentation
- 30-day email support
- Free updates for 6 months

Not included:
- White-label rights
- Resale rights
- Priority support

## Tier 2: Professional - $149

Best for: Small teams, startups

Includes:
- Everything in Starter, plus:
- Commercial license (up to 5 developers)
- White-label rights (internal use)
- 90-day priority email support
- Free updates for 1 year
- Setup assistance call (30 min)

## Tier 3: Enterprise - $499

Best for: Companies, agencies

Includes:
- Everything in Professional, plus:
- Unlimited developers
- Full white-label rights
- Resale rights (with attribution)
- 1-year support
- Custom integration assistance
- Onboarding call (2 hours)

Best for: SaaS founders

Includes:
- Everything in Enterprise, plus:
- Rebrand as your own product
- Remove MaryDubai branding
- Multi-tenant deployment rights
- Unlimited end-users
